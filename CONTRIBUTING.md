# Contributing to GLAZE UI

GLAZE UI V1.3 — Adaptive Resonance (`1.3.0`) is GoreeCloud's current Official, Stable, consumer-eligible shared visual and interaction design system. GLAZE UI V1.2 — Living Frosted (`1.2.0`) remains the immediately preceding known-good Stable rollback and audit release. V1.3.1 is the active unreleased hardening and qualification follow-up line; it is not Candidate, Release Candidate, Stable, consumer-eligible, or production-qualified merely because implementation or CI exists.

Current Stable work must preserve accessibility, semantic clarity, platform-native behavior, exact-revision evidence, consumer-governance boundaries, and GoreeCloud repository/revision-control requirements. V1.3.1 work must additionally preserve V1.3.0 Stable authority and V1.2 rollback provenance while completing the six transferred hardening and qualification obligations without relabeling missing evidence as passed.

Do not change `VERSION`, `registry/lifecycle.json`, official Candidate/Stable entrypoints, consumer eligibility, or downstream conformance as a side effect of ordinary implementation or qualification work. Lifecycle promotion requires a separate governed decision with fresh exact-revision evidence. Human and physical-device gates may be supported by automation, but automation must not impersonate the required review authority.

New V1.3.1 behavior should remain within governed follow-up or future Candidate namespaces until formal promotion and must be accompanied by the applicable contracts, tests, rendered/native evidence, accessibility coverage, migration/rollback considerations, and documentation. Downstream applications remain independently responsible for repository-local V1.3 conformance and production acceptance.

Git history remains the required revision audit trail. Historical release and Candidate records may retain their original version language when they are explicitly historical or provenance-only; current front-door guidance must always identify V1.3 / `1.3.0` as Stable until a later lifecycle promotion is formally recorded.
