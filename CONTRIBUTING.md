# Contributing to GLAZE UI

GLAZE UI V1.4 (`1.4.0`) is GoreeCloud's current Official, Stable, consumer-eligible shared visual and interaction design system. GLAZE UI V1.4.1 is the active planned follow-up / Candidate hardening workstream for contextual and capability awareness plus the human/manual/physical qualification explicitly deferred from V1.4.0. V1.4.1 work is not Release Candidate, Stable, consumer-eligible, or production-qualified merely because implementation or CI exists.

Current Stable work must preserve accessibility, semantic clarity, platform-native behavior, exact-revision evidence, consumer-governance boundaries, and GoreeCloud repository/revision-control requirements. V1.4.1 work must additionally preserve the V1.4.0 Stable rollback authority while implementing and qualifying the follow-up contracts and visual-quality requirements.

Do not change `VERSION`, `registry/lifecycle.json`, official Candidate/Stable entrypoints, consumer eligibility, or downstream conformance as a side effect of ordinary implementation or qualification work. Lifecycle promotion requires a separate governed decision with fresh exact-revision evidence. Human and physical-device gates may be supported by automation, but automation must not impersonate the required review authority.

New V1.4.1 behavior must remain within governed V1.4.1 Candidate/follow-up namespaces until formal promotion and must be accompanied by applicable contracts, tests, rendered/native evidence, accessibility coverage, migration/rollback considerations, and documentation. Downstream applications remain independently responsible for repository-local conformance and production acceptance.

Git history remains the required revision audit trail. Historical release and Candidate records may retain their original version language when they are explicitly historical or provenance-only; current front-door guidance must always derive lifecycle truth from `VERSION` and `registry/lifecycle.json`. At this revision, current Stable authority is GLAZE UI V1.4 / `1.4.0` and V1.4.1 remains a non-Stable follow-up until all governed acceptance gates are satisfied.
