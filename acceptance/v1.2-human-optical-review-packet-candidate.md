# GLAZE UI V1.2 Human Optical Review Packet — Candidate

## Status

**Lifecycle:** Candidate-only  
**Stable baseline:** GLAZE UI V1.1 / `1.1.0`  
**Human review:** Required and pending  
**Consumer eligibility:** No

This packet is a review handoff for G3 visual-quality evidence. It does not establish human optical acceptance, Release Candidate readiness, Stable promotion, production acceptance, or downstream consumer conformance.

## Exact-revision rule

Review only artifacts produced from one identical 40-character Candidate Git revision. The runtime manifest `artifacts/glaze-v1.2-human-optical-review-manifest.json` records that revision and the expected evidence workflows. If any artifact resolves to a different revision, the packet is invalid and review must fail closed.

## Required evidence classes

1. **Provisional Visual Regression** — paired rendered screenshots and zero-drift comparison evidence.
2. **Application Icon Ecosystem Wall** — canonical branding catalog, the pinned application-icon SVG sources, the review HTML, and its contract.
3. **Reference Scene Index** — the bounded V1.2 reference-scene sources and index contract.
4. **Living Frosted** — computed Clear/Balanced/Dense material evidence, bounded simple/complex backdrop response, wide/compact rendered screenshots, Navigation Capsule behavior, and accessibility fallbacks from the dedicated Living Glaze workflow.
5. **Exact-Head Readiness** — the fail-closed readiness report plus this packet manifest.

Machine-generated evidence is supporting material only. It cannot mark the human review complete.

## Human review dimensions

Review the exact-revision evidence for:

- light, dark, and Deep Dark optical consistency;
- desktop, mobile, and responsive composition;
- typography and large-text quality;
- interaction-state and feedback quality;
- Reduced Transparency and Increased Contrast quality;
- RTL layout quality;
- Clear, Balanced, and Dense Living Frosted differentiation without readability loss;
- Living Glaze interaction restraint, focus authority, perceived depth, and material continuity;
- Navigation Capsule reachability, hierarchy, context continuity, and compact-layout quality;
- application-icon family consistency;
- application-icon identity collisions or excessive similarity; and
- application-icon legibility at the bounded 96 px, 56 px, and 32 px review sizes.

## Recording an outcome

A human acceptance record, if later authorized, must identify the exact reviewed Candidate revision, reviewer, review time, evidence artifacts, findings, exceptions, and decision. It must be created as separate governed evidence. Do not edit machine-generated artifacts to manufacture approval.

Living Frosted rendered evidence is not self-accepting. A green Living Glaze workflow proves only its bounded automated checks and artifact production; the reviewer must still judge optical quality and usability on the exact Candidate revision.

Until that separate record exists and the governing acceptance contracts are intentionally advanced, all current human-review fields remain pending and all promotion claims remain false.

## Boundary

Successful assembly of this packet establishes only that the required machine evidence is organized for human review at one exact Candidate revision. G3 remains blocked until authorized human optical review is actually completed. G4 accessibility/performance acceptance and all downstream lifecycle/adoption gates remain separate.
