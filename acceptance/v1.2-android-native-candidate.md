# GLAZE UI V1.2 — Android Native Candidate Evidence

## Purpose

This acceptance track proves bounded framework-native Android handheld references for GLAZE UI V1.2 Frosted Neutral material behavior, Personalization / Living Frosted adapter behavior, consumer/platform adapter execution, deterministic window adaptation, and machine-observable accessibility structure. It preserves GLAZE UI V1.1 / 1.1.0 as current Stable authority.

Passing this track does not establish Release Candidate, Stable, production, downstream-consumer, physical-device, OEM-wide, assistive-technology, or complete native-platform acceptance.

## Candidate material contract

The Android reference follows the V1.2 governing rule: **Neutral glass is the material. Color is an accent.**

- Base, Raised, Overlay, Panel, and Critical System substrates are neutral-channel materials in Light, Dark, and Deep Dark.
- Light uses milky white translucent material over a neutral environmental field.
- Dark and Deep Dark use smoky neutral graphite glass rather than blue-green or teal substrate tint.
- GoreeCloud blue and semantic colors are reserved for active controls, focus, status, progress, selection, and identity.
- Reduced Transparency removes translucent-material dependence and resolves to opaque neutral surfaces.
- Critical System remains high-opacity and non-backdrop-dependent.
- Nested controls inside the dominant Control Center do not become independent blur authorities.

## Native reference coverage

`MainActivity` provides the bounded native material, System Shell, interaction, target-size, large-text, and Reduced Transparency scene.

`PersonalizationActivity` provides a bounded native adapter reference:

- Follow System resolves Android configuration at launch.
- manual Light, Dark, and Deep Dark override Follow System;
- Living Frosted Clarity normalizes to Clear, Balanced, or Dense and fails closed to Balanced;
- local RGB summary atmosphere remains low-intensity and layered over neutral material;
- Reduced Transparency suppresses decorative atmosphere;
- default targets remain at least 48 dp and Touch Assistance raises the floor to at least 56 dp.

`PlatformAdapterActivity` provides a bounded Android consumer/platform bridge:

- Android `WallpaperManager.getWallpaperColors(FLAG_SYSTEM)` may supply a system-owned color summary;
- raw wallpaper pixels are not read or transmitted;
- the reference consumer can exercise local `SharedPreferences` persistence independently of Glaze-core ownership;
- null platform summaries fail closed;
- cross-device preference synchronization remains outside this reference.

`FoldableAdaptationActivity` provides bounded window-size recomposition:

- `Configuration.screenWidthDp` drives compact/wide fixture recomposition;
- compact remains a complete single-panel environment;
- wide composition adds useful context rather than only scaling;
- optional hinge exclusion is producer-supplied fixture data;
- no hinge sensor, OEM posture API, or physical foldable claim is made.

## Automated emulator gates

The Android native workflow builds a fresh Candidate APK, creates a deterministic handheld emulator, installs the APK, and runs all gates against the exact workflow revision.

### Frosted Neutral runtime

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_runtime.py` validates neutral material assignments, Light/Dark/Deep Dark rendering, Reduced Transparency, 48/56 dp target floors, Quick Settings geometry, interaction state, screenshots, SDK/build fingerprint/density, and exact source revision.

### Personalization runtime

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_personalization_runtime.py` validates Follow System, manual appearance override, Clarity normalization, bounded local RGB summary atmosphere, Reduced Transparency precedence, invalid-input fail-closed behavior, 48/56 dp target floors, screenshots, and exact source revision.

### Consumer/platform adapter runtime

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_platform_adapter_runtime.py` validates bounded execution of Android system wallpaper-color summary APIs and reference-consumer local persistence while explicitly preserving Glaze-core ownership boundaries. It does not establish raw wallpaper acquisition, cross-device sync, physical-device, production, RC, or Stable acceptance.

### Window/foldable adaptation runtime

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_foldable_runtime.py` validates a compact 1080×2400 window fixture and a wide 2208×1840 fixture with producer-supplied hinge exclusion. It proves Android emulator window-size recomposition only; real hinge sensors, OEM posture semantics, and physical foldable qualification remain unestablished.

### Machine accessibility runtime

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_accessibility_runtime.py` is a fail-closed machine-observable accessibility gate. It validates:

1. exact source revision equality;
2. expected interactive controls expose accessible names;
3. Quick Settings controls expose state-bearing content descriptions;
4. expected controls expose native Android `Button` roles;
5. visible app clickables do not lose accessible names;
6. expected default targets remain at least 48 dp;
7. Touch Assistance targets remain at least 56 dp;
8. 200% text-scale task/content/control reachability in the bounded scene;
9. Reduced Transparency semantic-state reachability;
10. no accessibility service is owned or enabled by the bounded reference.

This machine hierarchy evidence does **not** establish TalkBack, Switch Access, Voice Access, human accessibility review, physical-device accessibility qualification, OEM accessibility-service interoperability, or complete native accessibility parity.

## Evidence outputs

A passing Android native workflow publishes:

- the debug Candidate APK;
- Frosted Neutral screenshots and `android-native-evidence.json`;
- Personalization screenshots and `android-personalization-evidence.json`;
- platform adapter screenshots and `android-platform-adapter-evidence.json`;
- compact/wide adaptation screenshots and `android-foldable-evidence.json`;
- machine accessibility hierarchy/target/reachability evidence in `android-accessibility-evidence.json`;
- per-case runtime JSON and screenshot hashes where applicable.

The exact-head readiness workflow separately publishes `glaze-v1.2-android-accessibility-source-readiness.json`. That source artifact verifies source/CI ownership only and cannot substitute for the emulator runtime gate.

## Boundaries

Passing this track is **Candidate emulator evidence only**. It does not establish:

- V1.2 Release Candidate designation or Stable promotion;
- human optical approval across Android OEM skins;
- OEM-wide or hardware-compositor blur fidelity;
- physical-device qualification;
- TalkBack, Switch Access, Voice Access, or other real assistive-technology acceptance;
- complete native-platform accessibility or form-factor parity;
- production consumer persistence authority;
- raw wallpaper-source acquisition acceptance;
- cross-device preference synchronization;
- real hinge/posture or physical foldable qualification;
- app signing or distribution acceptance;
- downstream GoreeCloud application conformance;
- production deployment eligibility.

Those remain separate release and adoption gates.
