# GLAZE UI V1.2 — Android Native Candidate Evidence

## Purpose

This acceptance track proves that the GLAZE UI V1.2 Candidate has bounded framework-native Android handheld references for Frosted Neutral material behavior and the Personalization / Living Frosted adapter boundary. It preserves GLAZE UI V1.1 / 1.1.0 as current Stable authority and does not establish Release Candidate, Stable, production, downstream-consumer, or physical-device acceptance.

## Candidate material contract

The Android Frosted Neutral reference follows the V1.2 material rule: neutral glass first, color second.

- Base, Raised, Overlay, and Panel substrates are neutral-channel materials in Light, Dark, and Deep Dark.
- Light uses milky white translucent material over a neutral wallpaper field.
- Dark and Deep Dark use smoky neutral graphite glass rather than blue-green or teal substrate tint.
- GoreeCloud blue and semantic colors are reserved for active controls, focus, status, progress, and identity.
- Reduced Transparency removes translucent material dependence and resolves to opaque neutral surfaces.
- Critical System remains high-opacity and non-backdrop-dependent.
- Nested controls inside the dominant Control Center do not become independent blur authorities.

## Native reference coverage

The framework-native Android reference includes:

- Frosted Neutral Candidate header and material contract summary.
- Candidate lifecycle and appearance evidence.
- Universal Search neutral material example.
- Quick Settings / Control Center with neutral tiles and accent-only active states.
- Critical System high-opacity example.
- Native primary and secondary actions.
- Light, Dark, and Deep Dark appearances.
- Reduced Transparency.
- Touch Assistance with a 56 dp minimum target.
- 200% text-scale reachability in the Deep Dark + Touch Assistance case.

A separate `PersonalizationActivity` provides a bounded native adapter reference for the V1.2 Extended Upgrade:

- Follow System resolves Light or Dark from Android configuration at launch.
- Manual Light, Dark, and Deep Dark preferences take precedence over system appearance.
- Living Frosted Clarity normalizes to Clear, Balanced, or Dense; invalid values fail closed to Balanced.
- Wallpaper-derived atmosphere accepts only a local RGB summary supplied through the adapter boundary.
- The reference does not acquire wallpaper pixels, invoke `WallpaperManager`, decode bitmaps, access a network, persist user settings, or synchronize preferences.
- Wallpaper atmosphere is intentionally low-intensity and bounded to a maximum alpha of 0.12 and maximum chroma retention of 0.28.
- Default action targets remain at least 48 dp and Touch Assistance raises the target floor to at least 56 dp.

## Automated emulator gates

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_runtime.py` runs against a freshly booted Android emulator and fails closed when the expected Frosted Neutral Candidate contract is not present.

It verifies:

1. Exact source revision equality with the workflow revision.
2. Neutral-channel Base, Raised, Overlay, Panel, and Critical System source assignments.
3. Candidate lifecycle and material-contract labels.
4. Light / neutral translucent material rendering.
5. Dark / Reduced Transparency opaque fallback.
6. Deep Dark / 200% text / Touch Assistance reachability.
7. 48 dp minimum action targets.
8. 56 dp Touch Assistance minimum action targets.
9. Quick Settings tile target geometry.
10. Basic native interaction state change.
11. PNG screenshot generation and SHA-256 evidence hashes.
12. Android SDK, build fingerprint, density, and source revision evidence capture.

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_personalization_runtime.py` runs in the same exact-revision emulator session and provides adapter evidence for the Personalization / Living Frosted boundary.

It verifies:

1. The reference source and manifest contain no `INTERNET` permission, `SharedPreferences`, `WallpaperManager`, bitmap pixel acquisition, socket/network, or WebView authority.
2. Follow System resolves Light with Android night mode disabled.
3. Follow System resolves Dark with Android night mode enabled.
4. Manual Deep Dark remains independent of system night mode.
5. Clear, Balanced, and Dense Clarity normalization is deterministic, with invalid input failing closed to Balanced.
6. Valid local RGB summaries produce bounded wallpaper atmosphere.
7. Invalid RGB summaries fail closed to no wallpaper atmosphere.
8. Default Personalization action geometry remains at least 48 dp.
9. Touch Assistance raises the Personalization action floor to at least 56 dp.
10. Exact source revision, SDK, build fingerprint, density, screenshots, and explicit evidence boundaries are recorded.

## Boundaries

Passing this track is **Candidate emulator evidence only**. It does not establish:

- V1.2 Release Candidate designation or Stable promotion;
- human optical approval across Android OEM skins;
- OEM-wide or hardware-compositor blur fidelity;
- physical-device qualification;
- TalkBack or other assistive-technology acceptance;
- consumer persistence behavior or persistence ownership;
- direct wallpaper-source acquisition acceptance;
- cross-device preference synchronization;
- app signing or distribution acceptance;
- downstream GoreeCloud application conformance;
- production deployment eligibility.

Those remain separate release and adoption gates.
