# GLAZE UI V1.2 Candidate Promotion Gates

**Lifecycle:** Candidate acceptance control  
**Stable baseline:** GLAZE UI V1.1 / `1.1.0`  
**Target:** `1.2.0-candidate`  
**Consumer eligible:** No

This record defines the blocking acceptance gates for moving the V1.2 Frosted Optical work from Candidate development toward RC, Stable release, and subsequent consumer migration. Passing a gate proves only that gate. It does not imply later lifecycle acceptance.

## G0 — Stable Baseline Integrity

**Blocks:** all V1.2 work if failed.

Required evidence:

- `VERSION` is still `1.1.0`.
- `registry/lifecycle.json.currentStable` and `currentOfficial` are still `1.1.0`.
- V1.2 Candidate remains `consumerEligible: false`.
- V1.1 Stable CSS/runtime/contracts remain present.
- the ordinary V1.1 validator still passes.

Failure means Candidate work must not proceed as though the baseline is trustworthy.

## G1 — Optical Foundation Contract

**Blocks:** rendered optical alignment.

Required evidence:

- exact initial optical palette references are machine-readable;
- Frost White is primary material;
- Ice Blue is primary atmosphere;
- Clear, Mist, Frost, Dense Frost, and Opaque Frost are all contracted;
- Frost, Ice, Crystal, and Content Aura families are contracted;
- 65–80% neutral, 15–25% frosted, and 3–10% Ice Blue distribution guidance is represented;
- Full, Reduced, and Minimal performance profiles are represented;
- teal/purple default atmosphere, neon, high-saturation glow, and other documented anti-patterns are prohibited.

Passing G1 does **not** prove that rendered Candidate surfaces already implement these values.

## G2 — Component and System Shell Contract Parity

**Blocks:** full experience implementation.

Required evidence:

- exact inherited 32-component catalog coverage;
- exact inherited five-region System Shell coverage;
- material budget remains one dominant Glaze panel plus at most three small floating Glaze controls;
- nested backdrop blur remains disabled by default;
- reading surfaces remain Solid/Raised by default;
- consequential decision surfaces remain high-opacity/readability-first;
- Universal Search remains an explicitly reviewed signature surface.

## G3 — Rendered Optical Acceptance

**Blocks:** RC readiness.

Required evidence on an exact Candidate revision:

- Light, Dark, and Deep Dark reference scenes;
- Frost White dominance without sterile pure-white flattening;
- Ice Blue used as restrained atmosphere rather than large saturated fill;
- Crystal/cool-white edge illumination reads as light rather than outline;
- depth remains legible through opacity, frost, blur, luminance, geometry, and shadow;
- gradients are subtle and purpose-bound;
- text stays sharp over frosted environments;
- legacy V1.1 teal/amber Aura compatibility is retired or explicitly mapped to the Frost/Ice Aura model;
- no muddy, over-blurred, oversaturated, washed-out, neon, flat, excessively glossy, or hard-to-read material passes review.

Automated screenshots alone are insufficient. Human optical review is required before this gate can support RC.

## G4 — Accessibility, Performance, and Resilience

**Blocks:** RC readiness.

Required evidence:

- Reduced Transparency produces polished opaque Frost White/Cloud Gray or graphite surfaces with no background blur;
- Increased Contrast strengthens functional boundaries and reduces decorative atmosphere;
- Forced Colors preserves platform-authoritative meaning;
- Reduced Motion removes nonessential travel/dynamic optical motion while preserving state and navigation;
- 200%+ text preserves critical content/actions;
- RTL works where applicable;
- keyboard, touch, pointer, directional, and assistive input behavior is validated where supported;
- Full, Reduced, and Minimal performance profiles preserve hierarchy, semantics, target size, and recognizable Glaze identity;
- unavailable blur/transparency/assets do not remove critical content or actions.

## G5 — RC Exact-Head Acceptance

**Blocks:** RC designation and Stable consideration.

Required evidence:

- G0–G4 all pass for the same exact candidate head or are rerun for the final head;
- required web/native/runtime checks pass;
- supported platform/form-factor evidence is current and source-pinned;
- visual regression authority is current;
- human optical review approves the exact candidate;
- migration and rollback records are current;
- known release-blocking defects are resolved;
- no stale review or validation evidence is reused after the candidate head changes.

G5 may justify an RC designation only through the applicable lifecycle procedure. It does not make V1.2 Stable.

## G6 — Stable Release Promotion

**Blocks:** production consumer migration.

This is a separate governed release action.

Required evidence:

- lifecycle approval for Stable is recorded;
- `VERSION`, lifecycle registry, Stable contract/acceptance record, immutable tag/release identity, and exact source revision agree;
- release artifacts are traceable to the exact accepted source;
- V1.1 remains preserved as a prior known-good release;
- V1.2 is explicitly made consumer-eligible only as part of the Stable promotion.

A commit, merged PR, green CI run, Candidate screenshot set, or RC by itself cannot satisfy G6.

## G7 — Consumer Migration Acceptance

**Applies separately to every consumer.**

Required evidence per consumer:

- exact V1.2 Stable release/source anchor;
- repository-local migration change;
- semantic/token/component mapping as applicable;
- rendered or native tests as applicable;
- accessibility and resilience tests;
- supported form-factor/input tests;
- performance evidence appropriate to the product;
- representative workflow review;
- rollback anchor to the last verified consumer revision;
- product-specific production acceptance.

No design-system evidence can automatically establish a consumer's G7 result.

## Gate ordering

`G0 → G1 → G2 → G3 → G4 → G5 → G6 → G7`

Where work proceeds in parallel, a later gate still cannot be considered passed until all prerequisite gates and exact-head requirements are satisfied.

## Current acceptance status

This file defines the gates; it does not mark them passed. Current Candidate evidence must be evaluated against each gate on the exact revision under review.
