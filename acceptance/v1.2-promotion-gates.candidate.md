# GLAZE UI V1.2 Candidate Promotion Gates

**Lifecycle:** Candidate acceptance control  
**Stable baseline:** GLAZE UI V1.1 / `1.1.0`  
**Target:** `1.2.0-candidate`  
**Consumer eligible:** No  
**Release theme:** Living Frosted

This record defines the blocking acceptance gates for moving the V1.2 Frosted Neutral + Living Frosted work from Candidate development toward RC, Stable release, and subsequent consumer migration. Passing a gate proves only that gate. It does not imply later lifecycle acceptance.

The governing material rule is: **Neutral glass is the material. Color is an accent.** The older Frosted Optical palette remains a bounded companion optical layer that extends Frosted Neutral; it does not authorize blue, teal, amber, or any other chromatic color to become the default glass substrate.

## G0 — Stable Baseline Integrity

**Blocks:** all V1.2 work if failed.

Required evidence:

- `VERSION` is still `1.1.0`.
- `registry/lifecycle.json.currentStable` and `currentOfficial` are still `1.1.0`.
- V1.2 Candidate remains `consumerEligible: false`.
- V1.1 Stable CSS/runtime/contracts remain present.
- the ordinary V1.1 validator still passes.

Failure means Candidate work must not proceed as though the baseline is trustworthy.

## G1 — Frosted Neutral + Living Frosted Material/Optical Contract

**Blocks:** rendered optical/material alignment.

Required evidence:

- `tokens/glaze-v1.2-frosted-neutral.candidate.json` remains the governing substrate authority;
- Base, Raised, Overlay, Panel, and Critical System material roles remain neutral-channel in Light, Dark, and Deep Dark;
- teal, green, aqua, amber, brand color, and semantic color are prohibited as default substrate material;
- color is reserved for active controls, focus, selection, progress, semantic status, icons, and product identity;
- depth is communicated before hue through blur, opacity, luminance, border reflection, geometry, shadow, and specular response;
- optional environmental aura remains outside the substrate and within governed low-alpha bounds;
- Clear, Balanced, and Dense Living Frosted clarity profiles are contracted;
- Living Frosted interaction response, perceived thickness, concentric geometry, and Tier 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 degradation semantics are contracted;
- the Frosted Optical companion palette remains subordinate to Frosted Neutral material authority;
- Clear, Mist, Frost, Dense Frost, and Opaque Frost companion optical levels remain available where applicable;
- documented anti-patterns such as chromatic default substrate, neon glow, excessive tint, excessive transparency, and readability-harming distortion remain prohibited.

Passing G1 does **not** prove human optical acceptance or native/physical-device parity.

## G2 — Component, System Shell, Interaction, and Adaptation Contract Parity

**Blocks:** full experience implementation.

Required evidence:

- exact inherited 32-component catalog coverage;
- exact inherited five-region System Shell coverage;
- material budget remains one dominant Glaze panel plus at most three small floating Glaze controls;
- nested backdrop blur remains disabled by default;
- reading surfaces remain Solid/Raised by default;
- consequential decision surfaces remain high-opacity/readability-first;
- Universal Search remains an explicitly reviewed signature surface;
- Living Frosted interaction states preserve semantic state and accessibility precedence;
- adaptive navigation preserves semantic destinations across compact, standard, expanded/workspace, and far-view compositions;
- primary tasks and important state survive responsive and foldable recomposition;
- compact environments remain complete rather than functioning as incomplete desktop crops;
- more space adds useful context instead of authorizing clutter.

## G3 — Rendered Human Optical Acceptance

**Blocks:** RC readiness.

Required evidence on an exact Candidate revision:

- Light, Dark, and Deep Dark reference scenes;
- neutral glass remains visually dominant without sterile pure-white flattening or chromatic substrate drift;
- any Ice Blue or other atmospheric color remains restrained environmental light/accent rather than a default material fill;
- specular/cool-white edge illumination reads as light rather than hard outline;
- depth remains legible through opacity, diffusion, blur, luminance, geometry, border reflection, and shadow;
- Living Frosted Clear/Balanced/Dense profiles remain distinct while preserving readability and hierarchy;
- interactive optical changes communicate state without becoming distracting, liquid-like, or glow-heavy;
- gradients are subtle and purpose-bound;
- text stays sharp over frosted environments;
- legacy V1.1 teal/amber Aura compatibility is retired or explicitly governed without violating Frosted Neutral substrate authority;
- no muddy, over-blurred, oversaturated, washed-out, neon, flat, excessively glossy, hard-to-read, or chromatically dominated material passes review.

Automated screenshots alone are insufficient. Human optical review is required before this gate can support RC.

## G4 — Accessibility, Performance, Resilience, and Native Evidence

**Blocks:** RC readiness.

Required evidence:

- Reduced Transparency produces polished opaque neutral white/gray or graphite surfaces with no background-blur dependence;
- Increased Contrast strengthens functional boundaries before chroma;
- Forced Colors preserves platform-authoritative meaning;
- Reduced Motion removes nonessential travel/dynamic optical motion while preserving state and navigation;
- 200%+ text preserves critical content/actions;
- RTL works where applicable;
- 48 px/dp default target floors and 56 px/dp Touch Assistance floors remain intact where applicable;
- keyboard, touch, pointer, directional, and assistive input behavior is validated where supported;
- machine-observable accessibility names/roles do not substitute for real screen-reader or human assistive-technology acceptance;
- Full/Reduced/Minimal and Living Glaze Tier 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 degradation preserve hierarchy, semantics, target size, focus, state, and task completion;
- unavailable blur/transparency/assets do not remove critical content or actions;
- bounded native/emulator evidence remains exact-revision and explicitly separate from physical-device/OEM qualification;
- representative constrained-device performance, power, thermal, compositor, and memory acceptance is completed before RC where applicable.

## G5 — RC Exact-Head Acceptance

**Blocks:** RC designation and Stable consideration.

Required evidence:

- G0–G4 all pass for the same exact candidate head or are rerun for the final head;
- required web/native/runtime checks pass;
- supported platform/form-factor evidence is current and source-pinned;
- visual regression authority is current;
- human optical review approves the exact candidate;
- assistive-technology/manual accessibility acceptance is current for the claimed support matrix;
- migration and rollback records are current;
- known release-blocking defects are resolved;
- no stale review or validation evidence is reused after the candidate head changes.

G5 may justify an RC designation only through the applicable lifecycle procedure. It does not make V1.2 Stable.

## G6 — Stable Release Promotion

**Blocks:** production consumer migration.

This is a separate governed release action.

Required evidence:

- lifecycle approval for Stable is recorded;
- `VERSION`, lifecycle registry, Stable contract/acceptance record, immutable tag/release identity, and exact source revision agree;
- release artifacts are traceable to the exact accepted source;
- V1.1 remains preserved as a prior known-good release;
- V1.2 is explicitly made consumer-eligible only as part of the Stable promotion.

A commit, merged PR, green CI run, Candidate screenshot set, emulator run, or RC by itself cannot satisfy G6.

## G7 — Consumer Migration Acceptance

**Applies separately to every consumer.**

Required evidence per consumer:

- exact V1.2 Stable release/source anchor;
- repository-local migration change;
- semantic/token/component mapping as applicable;
- rendered or native tests as applicable;
- accessibility and resilience tests;
- supported form-factor/input tests;
- performance evidence appropriate to the product;
- representative workflow review;
- rollback anchor to the last verified consumer revision;
- product-specific production acceptance.

No design-system evidence can automatically establish a consumer's G7 result.

## Gate ordering

`G0 → G1 → G2 → G3 → G4 → G5 → G6 → G7`

Where work proceeds in parallel, a later gate still cannot be considered passed until all prerequisite gates and exact-head requirements are satisfied.

## Current acceptance status

This file defines the gates; it does not mark them passed. Current Candidate evidence must be evaluated against each gate on the exact revision under review. Human optical review, assistive-technology acceptance, physical-device/native parity, production performance qualification, RC approval, Stable promotion, and downstream consumer conformance remain separate fail-closed gates.
