## Historical staged qualification rule

The earlier six-requirement Stable-promotion model was split across lifecycle stages so qualification was not circular:

- tracks 1–5 were pre-Candidate blockers intended to have accepted current evidence for one identical exact source revision before a governed Candidate review;
- track 6 was post-Candidate/pre-Stable, intended to run after Candidate activation and preserve provenance to the qualified Candidate revision;
- expired, failed, superseded, mismatched, missing or unresolved evidence remained blocking for its applicable stage;
- automation could validate evidence structure and accounting but could not impersonate human/manual/physical-device review;
- qualification readiness never activated Candidate or Stable automatically; and
- consumer conformance and downstream production acceptance remained separate.

The 2026-09-07 project-owner bounded Stable promotion superseded this staged model **as the lifecycle gate for V1.3.0 only**. It did not convert any unfinished track to passed evidence. The unfinished tracks are V1.3.1 maintenance/hardening scope.
