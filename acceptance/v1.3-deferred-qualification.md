## Staged qualification rule

The six Stable-promotion requirements are split across lifecycle stages so qualification is not circular:

- tracks 1–5 are **pre-Candidate** blockers and must have accepted current evidence for one identical exact source revision before a governed Candidate promotion can be reviewed;
- track 6 is **post-Candidate/pre-Stable**, must run only after Candidate activation, may target a later exact source revision, and must reference the qualified Candidate revision;
- all six requirements remain mandatory before governed Stable promotion;
- expired, failed, superseded, mismatched, missing or unresolved evidence remains blocking for its applicable stage;
- automation may validate evidence structure and accounting but may not impersonate human/manual/physical-device review;
- qualification readiness never activates Candidate or Stable automatically;
- consumer conformance and downstream production acceptance remain separate.
