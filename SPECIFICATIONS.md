## Stable V1.3 requirements

V1.3 preserves inherited semantic/protected-state authority, accessibility precedence, the canonical component catalog, System Shell semantics, bounded Glaze-region budgets, and the V1.2 Frosted/Living material foundation while adding adaptive/dynamic color, expressive shape, responsive typography, Living Material 2.0 behavior, reachability, adaptive navigation, multi-pane and large-screen composition, contextual intelligence, motion continuity, accessibility/resilience, Personalization boundaries, and signature component/reference coverage.

The governing material rule is: **Neutral glass remains the material foundation. Adaptive expression is contextual, bounded, and subordinate to meaning, accessibility, and task completion.**

## Acceptance boundary

V1.3.0 is Official, Stable, and consumer-eligible under the project-owner lifecycle decision recorded on 2026-09-08. Consumer eligibility does not automatically make any downstream GoreeCloud application conformant or production-ready. Each consumer must migrate to `1.3.0` and satisfy repository-local platform, accessibility, workflow, rollback, performance, and production acceptance requirements.

Human optical/icon review, manual assistive-technology qualification, physical-device/native-platform qualification, representative production-performance qualification, native Personalization adapter qualification, and Stable source-namespace/migration/rollback hardening remain V1.3.1 follow-up. Those unresolved items are not represented as passed V1.3.0 evidence.
